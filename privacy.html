Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Warrior Spirit Muay Thai & Kickboxing (“we”, “us”) runs this website and the gym at Next Gen Fitness, Mount Grace Road, Potters Bar EN6 1RB. This page explains what personal information we collect through the website, why, and what your choices are.

What we collect

If you send an enquiry (for example to book a free class): your name, phone number, email address and your message.

If you create an account or book personal training: your name, mobile number, email address, a password (stored only in scrambled form, never in plain text), and the details of your bookings, including any notes you add. If you choose “Continue with Google” we receive your name and email address from Google. We never see your Google password.

If you are a coach: your login details and anything you upload to the coach area, such as profile text and photos.

Photos and videos: the gym shares photos and videos of training and fight nights. We only publish images of children if their parent or guardian is happy for them to be used. If you would rather not appear, you can ask to be left out in your account (choose “Please leave me out” under Photos and videos), or tell a coach. A parent’s choice in their account covers their child too. We keep that choice with your account, with the date you made it, and show the coaches the names of the people who have asked, so they know who to leave out. You can change your mind at any time.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, to run bookings and accounts, to email you confirmations and reminders about your booking, to keep our members and coaches safe and to run the gym. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Our legal reasons are: to carry out what you have asked us to do (your enquiry or booking), our legitimate interest in running the gym and keeping the website secure, and your consent where we ask for it (for example the tick box on the enquiry form).

Who handles it for us

We use trusted services to run the site. They only process your information on our behalf:

  • Vercel, which hosts the website and stores uploaded files;
  • Upstash, which stores our booking and account database;
  • Google, which sends our emails through Gmail, provides the optional “Continue with Google” sign-in, and stores photos and videos that coaches upload to Google Drive;
  • Cloudflare, whose Turnstile check helps stop automated spam and attacks on our forms and sign-in (see “Checking you’re a person” below).

Some of these services are based outside the UK, mainly in the EU and the USA, and rely on approved safeguards for transfers.

Keeping accounts safe

To protect accounts from password guessing and the forms from spam, we count sign-in, sign-up, password-reset and enquiry attempts by email address, phone number and internet (IP) address. These counts are deleted automatically within a day. Our hosting provider also keeps short-lived technical records of visits (such as the IP address and the page asked for) to keep the site running and secure.

Checking you’re a person

When you send an enquiry, create an account, log in or ask to reset your password, we use Cloudflare Turnstile to check you’re a person and not a bot. It looks at information from your browser and connection, such as your IP address, your browser and device details and how you use the page. Usually you won’t see anything; sometimes you’ll be asked to tick a box. We do this to keep the site, accounts and emails safe from automated spam and attacks (our legitimate interests). We don’t keep the result.

Cloudflare also uses some of this information to improve its bot detection, as a separate controller. Its Turnstile Privacy Addendum explains how: cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy.

We also check enquiries for spam automatically. One sent faster than a person could fill it in, or that looks like a sales pitch, is kept for the club to read but isn’t emailed to them. Nothing is deleted automatically, and the club checks these messages.

Cookies

We use only the cookies the site needs to work, mainly to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

When you send an enquiry, sign up, log in or reset your password, the bot check may store a small piece of information on your device so it works. It’s used only for security, not for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and booking records for as long as we need them to run the gym and answer questions, and then delete them. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to object to how we use it, or to take a copy of it. To do any of these, email us at the address below. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
