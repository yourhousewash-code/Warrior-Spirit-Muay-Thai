Accessibility statement

Last updated 2 November 2026

We want everyone to be able to use this website. This statement applies to warriorspiritmuaythai.co.uk.

What we do

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 level AA. The site is written in plain HTML, works with a keyboard, uses headings and labels so screen readers can find their way, and sets its language to British English. Text is set to be readable against its background, and the site works on phones and larger screens.

How accessible it is

We have not yet had the site independently tested, so we describe it as partly checked rather than fully compliant. These are the problems we know about:

  • Some videos have no captions or written transcript yet. Many clips are short and silent.
  • Some photos have short descriptions that could be fuller.
  • Parts of the booking and account pages have had less checking than the main pages.

We are working through these. Where we can, we will add captions and better descriptions to new videos and photos.

Need something in another way?

If you cannot use part of the site, or you need information in another format, tell us and we will help, for example by phone or by booking you in ourselves. We aim to reply within 5 working days.
